# ZhiShi Short Video Platform - Complete User Guide

## Platform Overview

ZhiShi (知识) is a short video sharing platform focused on knowledge sharing and creative content. Users can upload, browse, search, and interact with short videos across multiple categories. The platform features an AI-powered assistant named "小知" (XiaoZhi) that helps users navigate the platform, discover content, and get creative suggestions.

### Key Features
- Video upload and sharing (standard video, images, panoramic/360° content)
- Smart video search with keyword, hashtag, and category filters
- Trending topics and hot content discovery
- Social interactions: likes, comments, follows, and shares
- Personalized content recommendations
- AI-powered assistant for platform guidance and content strategy
- Multi-format content support: MP4, MOV, AVI video; JPEG, PNG, GIF images
- User profiles with customizable bio, avatar, and content showcase

## User Registration and Login

### How to Register
1. Open the ZhiShi app or visit the web platform
2. Click "Register" (注册) on the login page
3. Enter your email address or phone number
4. Create a strong password (at least 8 characters, including letters and numbers)
5. Complete the verification process (email or SMS code)
6. Set up your profile: choose a username, upload an avatar, and write a short bio
7. You're ready to explore!

### How to Login
1. Open the ZhiShi app or web platform
2. Enter your registered email/phone and password
3. Click "Login" (登录)
4. If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, enter the verification code

### Forgot Password
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your registered email or phone number
3. You will receive a reset link or verification code
4. Create a new password and confirm it

## Video Upload

### How to Upload a Video
1. Click the "+" button on the bottom navigation bar
2. Select video from gallery or record a new one directly
3. Add a descriptive title (up to 100 characters recommended)
4. Write a detailed description to help others discover your content
5. Add relevant tags/hashtags (e.g., #coding #tutorial #tech)
6. Select a category for your video (e.g., Education, Entertainment, Tech, Lifestyle)
7. Choose visibility: public, private, or friends-only
8. Click "Publish" to upload

### Supported File Formats
- **Video**: MP4 (recommended), MOV, AVI, WebM
- **Images**: JPEG, PNG, GIF (for image posts)
- **Panoramic/360°**: MP4 with 360° metadata
- **Maximum file size**: 500MB for standard uploads, 2GB for premium users
- **Recommended resolution**: 1080p (1920x1080) or higher
- **Recommended aspect ratio**: 9:16 (vertical) for best mobile viewing

### Upload Tips
- Use good lighting and stable camera for better quality
- Keep videos between 15 seconds and 3 minutes for optimal engagement
- Add subtitles/captions to improve accessibility
- Use eye-catching thumbnails to attract viewers

## Video Search

### Basic Search
- Use the search bar at the top of the home page
- Type keywords related to the content you're looking for
- Press Enter or tap the search icon to see results

### Advanced Search Features
- **Keyword search**: Find videos by title, description, or tags
- **Hashtag search**: Type # followed by a tag name (e.g., #cooking)
- **User search**: Type @ followed by a username to find creators
- **Category filter**: Filter results by content category (Education, Entertainment, Tech, etc.)
- **Date range filter**: Find content from specific time periods
- **Popularity sort**: Sort results by views, likes, or recent uploads
- **Search results** are ranked by relevance and user engagement

### Search Tips
- Use specific keywords for better results
- Combine multiple search terms for more precise results
- Use quotation marks for exact phrase matching
- Check trending hashtags for popular topics

## Hot Topics and Trending

### How Trending Works
- The "Trending" (热门) section shows currently popular content
- Hot score is calculated based on a weighted formula:
  - Views: 1 point each
  - Likes: 3 points each
  - Comments: 5 points each
  - Shares: 8 points each
  - Time decay: Recent interactions are weighted more heavily
- Trending videos are updated every hour
- Different time windows available: 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours, 7 days

### Exploring Trending Content
- Navigate to the "Trending" tab on the home page
- Browse trending topics organized by category
- View "Rising Fast" content that's gaining momentum quickly
- Check the "Weekly Picks" section for editorial highlights

## Social Interactions

### Likes
- Tap the heart icon on any video to like it
- Double-tap the video while watching to quickly like it
- View all your liked videos in your profile under "Likes" tab
- Likes are public by default; you can make them private in Settings

### Comments
- Tap the comment icon below any video to view or add comments
- Write your comment and tap "Send"
- You can reply to other users' comments by tapping "Reply"
- Mention other users with @username in your comment
- Comments support emoji and text formatting
- You can pin your own comments on your videos
- Report inappropriate comments via the "..." menu

### Following and Followers
- Tap the "Follow" button on any user's profile to follow them
- View your following list and followers list in your profile
- Turn on "Notifications" for specific creators to get alerts when they post
- You can block users to prevent them from viewing your content or contacting you

### Sharing
- Tap the share icon to share videos
- Share options: Copy link, share to other apps, send to friends on platform
- Share counts contribute to the video's trending score

## Content Recommendations

### How Recommendations Work
The platform uses a combination of algorithms to recommend content:
1. **Collaborative filtering**: Based on what similar users enjoy
2. **Content-based filtering**: Based on your viewing history and preferences
3. **Trending signals**: Popular content gets boosted in recommendations
4. **Creator diversity**: System ensures you see content from various creators
5. **Freshness**: New content gets a temporary boost

### Improving Your Recommendations
- Like and interact with content you enjoy
- Follow creators whose content interests you
- Use the "Not Interested" option on content you don't want to see
- Explore different categories to broaden your feed

## FAQ

### How to Get More Views on Your Videos?
1. **Use relevant and popular hashtags** - Research trending tags in your category
2. **Post during peak hours** - Best times: 12:00-13:00 (lunch), 18:00-20:00 (evening peak), weekends 10:00-12:00
3. **Create engaging thumbnails and titles** - First impressions matter; use clear, compelling titles
4. **Interact with your audience** - Reply to comments, ask questions, create polls
5. **Collaborate with other creators** - Cross-promotion helps reach new audiences
6. **Maintain a consistent posting schedule** - Regular uploads keep your audience engaged
7. **Use trending sounds and formats** - Participate in popular trends and challenges
8. **Optimize video length** - 30-60 seconds tends to perform best for most categories

### How to Report Inappropriate Content?
1. Click the "..." (more options) button on the video
2. Select "Report" (举报)
3. Choose the reason for reporting:
   - Spam or misleading content
   - Harassment or bullying
   - Hate speech or discrimination
   - Violence or graphic content
   - Nudity or sexual content
   - Copyright infringement
   - Misinformation
   - Other
4. Provide additional details if necessary
5. Submit the report
6. Our moderation team reviews reports within 24 hours

### Account Security Best Practices
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) in Settings > Security
- Use a strong, unique password (mix of letters, numbers, special characters)
- Never share your login credentials with anyone
- Review active sessions regularly in Settings > Security > Active Sessions
- Log out from devices you no longer use
- Be cautious of phishing links and suspicious messages
- Update your password regularly (at least every 6 months)

### How to Delete My Account?
1. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
2. Read the account deletion information
3. Enter your password to confirm
4. Your account will be deactivated for 30 days (during which you can recover it)
5. After 30 days, all data will be permanently deleted

### How to Manage Privacy Settings?
1. Go to Settings > Privacy
2. Available privacy options:
   - **Account visibility**: Public or Private account
   - **Who can comment**: Everyone, Followers only, or No one
   - **Who can message**: Everyone, Followers only, or No one
   - **Who can see my likes**: Everyone, Only me
   - **Location sharing**: Enable or disable location tags on videos
   - **Download permission**: Allow or disallow others from downloading your videos

### What File Formats Are Supported for Upload?
- **Video**: MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV, AVI, WebM
- **Image**: JPEG, PNG, GIF (animated), WebP
- **Audio**: Supported within video files (AAC, MP3)
- **Maximum file size**: 500MB (standard), 2GB (premium)
- **Maximum duration**: 10 minutes (standard), 30 minutes (premium)
